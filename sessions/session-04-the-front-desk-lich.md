# Session 04 — The Front-Desk Lich

**Date played:** 2026-09-10
**Real-world session length:** ~153 minutes recorded (~2h33m)
**In-game date:** Straight on from Session 03 — a short rest in the temple barracks, then roughly an hour working through the Amber Temple's upper floor.

## TL;DR
The party cleared the barracks they'd shut the door on last session, took the room for a short rest, and spent the next hour looting the Amber Temple's upper floor — a Tome of Understanding, a wine-making ewer, a potion of alchemist's fire — while walking face-first into a hospitality illusion, a statue that charms you into starving to death, and a ceiling chest that dropped Bulbin thirty feet into a room of poltergeists. They ended the night meeting a lich who turned out to be the friendliest thing in the building.

## What happened
- Picks up in the entrance hall over the dead guardian. The DM opens with "there's no other bad guys inside this place," and the table immediately catches it: "**he said the *first* enemy.**" Some debate about burning the corpse to make sure it stays dead; the DM points out it isn't human, so they could eat it. Phoenix Cloak declines on principle: "I only eat the rich people."
- Casimir wants a short rest but it's freezing, and the six armed strangers Bulbin spotted last session are still behind their door. The party reasons it out: "**if we beat the shit out of these people, we get their room for sleeping.**"
- Prep: someone casts **Aid** on three of them (+5 max HP and 5 healing for 8 hours), and the plan is to come through the door and the broken arrow slit at once. The DM rules only one attacker gets surprise, because the door is loud.
- **A confession from behind the screen.** The DM admits he went easy in Session 03: the berserkers should have heard the guardian fight and joined in. "I was just being nice last game... **because I think y'all would have actually died.**"
- **The barracks fight.** Casimir opens the door and leads with **Ice Storm** (4th level, 20-foot square, difficult terrain) — only two of the enemies save, which decides the whole fight before it starts. Inside: a pack of berserkers and gladiators plus a **dire wolf**.
  - Dog rages, squeezes through the slit, and kills the dire wolf on the first of three claw attacks.
  - Dablin drops a **Fireball** through the open door, then later lands a **natural 20 Firebolt** for 21: "well, at least now you'll be warmed up."
  - Bulbin rages and spears his way through, critting his **Divine Fury** for radiant damage and picking up temp HP on each kill. Kill described as "spear through the eyeball."
  - Parriwimple decapitates one, mounts the head on his spear, and presents it: "look at what I got."
  - Phoenix Cloak runs his hide-then-sneak-attack routine all fight. Mid-combat the DM realizes he's been carrying a **+2 rapier since the bridge in Session 02 and has never once used it** — it was never added to his sheet. They agree to fix it during the short rest.
  - Scottishbard's Vicious Mockery target saves ("15 meets and beats"), so he spends the fight handing out Bardic Inspiration instead.
  - The last one dies to a thrown spear from Parriwimple. The party takes the room.
- **The Amber Temple's actual gimmick, explained.** It is **−10°C** inside. Anyone without serious cold gear makes **Constitution saving throws**, and there's a visible **60-minute clock**: every search or check burns **5 minutes**, and asking for advantage on a check costs 5 minutes by itself. When the clock hits 60, everyone saves again. Exempt: Casimir (his ring), Bulbin (the bear pelt from Session 02), and Dog (a furry, per the table's ruling). Phoenix Cloak's shop-bought blanket covers him for the rest but nothing else.
- The DM reveals the other half of the trap they avoided: if they'd short rested anywhere but in that room, he'd have **moved the two barbarians to a random location** on the map without telling anyone.
- **The short rest.** Everyone spends Hit Dice; Scottishbard's **Song of Rest** (d6 until 9th level) tops it up. Several people admit to overspending dice. Casimir hits full health off one roll. Dablin uses **Arcane Recovery** to get a spell slot back. Phoenix Cloak asks how short rests work, gets walked through Hit Dice plus Constitution modifier, and finally adds the +2 rapier to his sheet — it ends up at **+9 to hit**.
- **Working the upper floor**, five minutes at a time:
  - The **dire wolf gets skinned** (survival check, with help, with advantage) — another pelt, and whoever wears it is now safe from the cold.
  - A storeroom of broken barrels with the floor stained red from spilled wine. Nothing in it.
  - The **Wand of Secrets** — 30-foot range, pulses and points at the nearest secret — turns up a **secret door** onto a hallway and a staircase heading down.
  - An old **potion workshop**, mostly dried up. Dablin takes charge on the strength of **Artificer's Lore** (double proficiency on History for magic items and alchemy), rolls a **natural 20**, and finishes a half-made **potion of alchemist's fire**. "It's basically like a molotov."
  - A workshop of plain, practical tools — whoever lived here was living off the land. Sitting in it is a **replica of Strahd's castle**, recognized on a perception check only because the party walked past the real thing on the road. A chest nearby holds a map case whose maps have rotted to dust — but a **passive perception of 18** catches a noise from a **hidden false bottom**, and inside is a **Tome of Understanding**: a permanent Wisdom increase. The DM, relieved: "I would have been really sad if y'all missed this item."
- **The dining room.** A room that smells incredible — piping hot gravy, vegetables, cooked meat, wine — with a green copper **ewer** in the middle, figured with dancing bears, elks and wolves. It is far too cozy for a building where everything else is dead. Casimir's Detect Magic comes back **transmutation**, not illusion, which is the wrong answer and nobody can explain why. The party debates it, decides it's probably a trap, and **Parriwimple grabs it anyway** — the whole room dissolves and the specters underneath it come out.
  - The ghosts are **immune to charm**, which would have gutted Scottishbard's kit if he hadn't already swapped those spells out "ever since the wolves that one day."
  - Dog's claws count as magical, so he can actually hurt them. Bulbin discovers the hard way that **rage comes back on a long rest, not a short one** — "**raging has now become a commodity in this dungeon.**"
  - Phoenix Cloak's newly-configured rapier gets its first outing and kills one: "I poke him in the belly, he dies."
  - The DM forgets to roll initiative for Casimir *and* Parriwimple. Again.
  - The ewer, identified afterwards, **turns water and other things into wine**. Given Bulbin's entire character, this lands about as well as anything could: "infinite wine."
- **The skull room.** A room packed wall-to-wall with skulls, unlit candlesticks, a small knight statue, and **two corpses lying at its feet**. Walking in calls for a **Wisdom save**; failing means you simply want to look at the statue, forever. Bulbin fails and fixates — "man, this statue just looks so cool" — and the party works out what the two corpses are: people who stood there **until they starved to death**. Scottishbard breaks the line of sight with a **Minor Illusion** plank, and then someone makes it permanent by throwing a blanket over the thing.
- **The chest on the ceiling.** With the statue covered they can search the skulls, and spot an **iron chest fixed to the ceiling** — held there with **sovereign glue** and sealed with an **arcane lock**. Mage Hand can't shift it (20-pound limit). A strength check fails; a **Bardic Inspiration die turns the failure into a success** — and the floor of the room disintegrates.
  - Scottishbard catches the chest lid and hangs there. **Bulbin falls thirty feet**, raging on the way down, and lands in a dark room full of broken swords with a pack of **poltergeists** — on a separate map, alone, while everyone else watches from the hole above and can only hear clanging.
  - The chest is **empty**. "So why did I spend all this time trying to open up the chest?" — "Because you thought there was something in it."
  - Bulbin kills the lot of them single-handed: "**I'm not stuck in a room with them. They're stuck in a room with me.**" The party hauls him back up on a rope, over his own objection that he weighs 400 pounds. (The DM upgrades this to 500.)
- **The lich.** Across the gap is another secret door. Behind it is a small figure with a **rotting face and flaming eyes** — unmistakably a **lich** — who greets them pleasantly and asks what they're doing here. He is completely uninterested in Strahd ("sounds like a cool idea, I guess"), can't remember who he is, and describes himself as **the front desk librarian**. He can't leave; he says it isn't a magical compulsion, just a feeling, like he had a duty here once.
  - He volunteers the thing the party came for: **there's a lot of power here, and that's how he got his**. Downstairs on the second level there are **sarcophagi** — "you just touch them, you should be able to get powers." He can't remember what kind.
  - He offers to show them the **library**, opens the doors, and casually **conjures ground** across the pit for them to walk on. There's a **spell book** visible in his room.
  - Dog whispers to Casimir: "**We are so fucked.**" Casimir, a dusk elf, "somehow turns even more pale." Parriwimple, reading the room as ever: "What are you guys looking at? Hey man, I think you need some lotion." Casimir puts a hand over his mouth and tells him to shut up.
- Session ends there, mid-introduction. The DM, delighted: "That was something I was very excited to do. I was hoping y'all would make it to that."

## Key decisions
- Cleared the barracks instead of resting elsewhere — which, unknowingly, was the right call twice over: it got them a defensible room, and resting anywhere else would have scattered the barbarians across the map.
- Led with Casimir's Ice Storm rather than a Fireball, specifically to halve enemy movement and protect the back line. It decided the fight.
- Grabbed the ewer knowing it was probably a trap, because they wanted to know what the transmutation magic was.
- Covered the charming statue with a blanket rather than trying to destroy it.
- Spent real time and two characters' safety opening a ceiling chest that turned out to be empty — and afterwards agreed to just ask the DM whether a room has anything good in it.
- Did **not** attack the lich. Universally agreed afterwards to be the only reason anyone is alive.

## Combat / danger
- **The barracks** — berserkers, gladiators and a dire wolf. Opened with Ice Storm; only two enemies saved, and the party cleared it without anyone dropping. Dog absorbed 34 damage in one round and halved it with rage.
- **The dining room specters** — sprung deliberately by grabbing the ewer. Necrotic damage and repeated Constitution saves; immune to charm, vulnerable to magical weapons. No one went down.
- **The pit** — Bulbin fell 30 feet and fought a room of poltergeists alone, on a separate map, with no line of sight to the rest of the party. Won it outright.
- **The statue** — no damage, but a save-or-stand-here-until-you-die effect, with two corpses on hand as proof it works.
- Standing hazard: the cold. −10°C, Constitution saves every 60 minutes of in-fiction time, and **Dog took a level of exhaustion** from it.

## Loot & discoveries
- **Tome of Understanding** — permanent Wisdom increase, from a false bottom in a chest that was nearly missed entirely.
- **The ewer** — turns water and other things into wine. Taken off the dining room trap.
- **Potion of alchemist's fire** — crafted by Dablin on a natural 20 from leftovers in the potion workshop.
- **Dire wolf pelt** — skinned from the barracks fight; cold protection for whoever wears it.
- **Phoenix Cloak's +2 rapier** — not new, but functionally new: he's had it since Session 02 and only put it on his sheet this session. Now +9 to hit.
- A **replica of Strahd's castle** in the workshop, and an empty map case whose contents have rotted away.
- **Lore, straight from the lich:** the power in this temple comes from **sarcophagi on the level below**, and touching one grants something. He doesn't remember what, or who he is, or why he can't leave.

## NPCs encountered
- **The lich** *(new — not hostile)* — rotting face, flaming eyes, small, and disarmingly friendly. Calls himself the front desk librarian; remembers almost nothing about himself. Got his own power from the temple's sarcophagi. Offered to show the party the library and conjured ground across a pit for them. Not named yet.
- **Casimir** — opened the barracks fight with Ice Storm, cast Detect Magic on the ewer, and is exempt from the cold thanks to his ring. Went visibly pale at the lich.
- **Parriwimple** — mounted a berserker's head on his spear, sprang the dining room trap by grabbing the ewer, and told the lich he needs lotion.
- **Berserkers, gladiators and a dire wolf** *(killed)* — the six-plus armed strangers Bulbin shut the door on in Session 03. They were sharpening weapons for a reason.
- **Specters / poltergeists** *(killed)* — two separate groups: the ones hiding under the dining room illusion, and the ones waiting at the bottom of the pit.

## Fallen
No deaths. Closest thing to a casualty: Dog picked up **one level of exhaustion** from the cold, and Bulbin spent a chunk of the night alone at the bottom of a hole.

## Cliffhanger / where we left off
Mid-conversation with a friendly lich who has just opened the doors to a library and conjured a floor across the pit so the party can walk over. He has told them the temple's power is in sarcophagi on the level below, and that touching one grants something he can't remember. Nobody has attacked anybody. The general table read is that this holds exactly as long as they stay polite.

## DM notes / questions for next time
- **Does the lich have a name?** He doesn't remember it. Worth asking whether that's findable.
- What are the **sarcophagi** on the level below, and what is the actual cost of touching one? He said it's how he got his power, which is not a reassuring precedent given that he's a lich who can't remember his own name or leave the building.
- The ewer read as **transmutation rather than illusion** and nobody worked out why. Still unexplained.
- The **60-minute cold clock** is now the main pressure in this dungeon — worth tracking how much of it gets spent per room, and who still has no cold gear. Dog is already at one level of exhaustion.
- There's a **spell book** in the lich's room. Nobody has asked about it.
- The staircase down found with the Wand of Secrets is still unexplored, as are several doors on this floor.
- Settled this session: **Cassius was a paladin**, per Todd comparing Phoenix Cloak's rogue to his old character. The Session 01 write-up called him a wizard — corrected.
