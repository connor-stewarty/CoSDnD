# Session 03 — Into the Amber Temple

**Date played:** 2026-08-27
**Real-world session length:** ~139 minutes recorded (~2h19m)
**In-game date:** Picks up the evening after the gate fight — a long rest in the tower past the gate, then the following morning arriving at the Amber Temple and fighting through its front hall.

## TL;DR
The party rested in the tower past the statue gate, left Irina and Rudolph van Richten behind with the fire, and finally reached the Amber Temple — where a hooded, eyeless statue in the entrance hall turned out to be a spellcasting guardian who opened with Fireball, banished Dog to a nightmare dimension, dropped Scottishbard with Chain Lightning, and nearly killed him again with Finger of Death, before Bulbin ended it with a thrown spear.

## What happened
- Session opens where Session 02 left off: past the gate, in a stone tower. Someone kicks the door in — latches broken, the door falls, revealing a staircase — and the party decides to rest.
- Fire-starting goes badly. Tinderbox out, survival check comes back an 8: the wood is damp, it's freezing, and the match "burns down to my fingertips" and is described as being like a popsicle. After some heavy hinting ("if only someone had, like, magic that specifically does that... if only someone had Firebolt"), **Dablin Horkus Porkus** casts Firebolt for 10 fire damage and the fire takes. The table narrates it as a Dark Souls checkpoint: "*bonfire lit* appears across the screen."
- Upstairs, the trap door to the top floor is frozen shut. It takes an athletics check (12) and opens as loudly as physically possible — the whole thing shatters and every piece of metal bounces three separate times on the floor below. The top floor holds **stone statues of soldiers in full plate armor** and is brutally cold. A perception check (20) turns up nothing suspicious: the bridge outside is broken but solid enough to cross, and the statues don't look like they'd come alive — though, as noted, "the other ones also didn't look like they would come alive."
- The party takes a **long rest** on the warmer first floor. Parriwimple and the wizard fall asleep instantly and snore through it.
- In the morning, Casimir offers an out: the temple will be perilous and it only gets colder from here, so anyone not cut out for it should stay in the warm tower. **Irina takes it** — she nearly died in one swipe at the gate and knows she can't hold anything off. The party leaves **Rudolph van Richten** with her, over some out-of-character grumbling that he's their only healer (verdict: "pretty trash healing," and the bard can do it). He agrees to keep her safe and keep the fire going. Irina turns down rations.
- **The Amber Temple.** The road disappears entirely under snow. The temple is built straight into the mountainside, around 50 feet high, with about six enclaves along its face, each holding a small statue of a hooded figure, all of them praying. Stairways run up the middle; rubble and debris everywhere. It is savagely cold — Bulbin reckons that without the bear pelt from Session 02 he'd already be dead.
- Dog scouts a broken gap in the wall (perception 21) and spots **a wolf denned up inside**. Extended table argument about whether wolves count as Dog's family ("those are two completely different things, man"); Dog's ruling is that this one "doesn't smell very friendly." The party leaves it alone and goes in the front.
- Inside the entrance hall: a long hallway, a staircase, a ledge, and something big with **hands** at the far end. The walls have arrow slits. Dog rolls a **natural 20** looking for traps — no traps, but the slits have decayed enough that a small creature could squeeze through them into the rooms behind.
- Left slit room: completely empty, picked clean. Right slit room: **a skeleton with a wand lying next to it**. Dog claws through, checks the body first (perception 12 — nothing wrong with it), grabs the wand in his mouth, squeezes back out, and hands it straight to Dablin.
- Dablin ritual-casts Identify (10 minutes) and confirms it: a **Wand of Secrets**. Verdict: "this is so much better than the find traps spell."
- At the end of the hall stands a much larger version of the hooded statues outside. A general perception check (14) says it "looks a little off" — vibes only, eerie rather than gut-wrenching. Comparing it to the ones outside turns up two real differences: **its hands are open, as if waiting for an offering**, while the others are praying — and **it has no eyes**.
- Someone steps in closer, and the DM announces they've "activated the funny." Up close you can see why there are no eyes: there is **pure pitch darkness inside the sockets**. A bolt of light comes out of that darkness, and it's a **Fireball**. The party is caught surprised; everyone except Phoenix Cloak and Casimir is in the blast.
- A long rules argument follows about danger sense, rage and surprise, and whether you can see a fireball coming out of a statue's eye socket. The DM concedes he ran the order wrong — the party should have rolled initiative before the fireball landed — can't un-ring the bell, and lets everyone act anyway. ("I made a booboo.") Counterspell's range gets litigated in the same breath and settles at 60 feet.
- **The fight, roughly:**
  - Dog rages and dashes 45 feet at the statue, which is 40 feet tall — about 35 feet up to the eyes. With nothing in reach to hit, he **punches himself for full damage to stay raged** ("I hurt myself today") and starts climbing.
  - **Flaming skulls** boil out of the second-floor arrow slits, shooting fire from their mouths. They're immune to fire and each one has Fireball.
  - Scottishbard turns invisible and wanders off to explore instead — finds a staircase. He later fails a 3rd-level Dispel Magic on the statue's hood and hands Bulbin a Bardic Inspiration die.
  - Bulbin can't reach the arrow slits from the ground, runs upstairs, opens a door and finds **six armed people awake and sharpening weapons** — and closes it again before they notice (acrobatics check). The DM is visibly relieved: "I would have had to do a three-faction battle and my brain would have actually died."
  - Bulbin then tries a running jump at a window, misses, and slides all the way back down the wall. Over the following rounds he climbs it properly, punches his own handholds through the stonework, gets *inside* an arrow slit, and kills two flaming skulls — one on a natural 20.
  - **Dog is banished.** A failed charisma save (a 15, with a -2 modifier — "very good, but not good enough for this") and a voice asking *"You want to see your nightmares?"* drops him into a dimension where every single dog is eating a bone except him. He picks a fight with one of them, kills it ("I scratched it so hard it vaporized into dust"), and takes its bone — which has **a plus sign inside it** (medicine check, 21: it'll heal someone who chews on it). He pops back into the fight a minute later, still 15 feet up the statue.
  - Casimir casts Dispel Magic on the hood and fails: "That hood — we literally can't see through it. There's a reason why." Dablin confirms it's **magical darkness**, and can't do anything about it either.
  - Phoenix Cloak plays it exactly right all fight: hide with the cunning action, sneak attack with the shortbow, repeat. He crits a flaming skull for 34, later rolls a **29 on stealth** ("I don't even know anything that has a 30 passive perception — maybe like a Tarrasque"), crits again, and gets to narrate the kill himself: the arrow finds the right spot, the skull cracks, and it blows apart in green flame.
  - The guardian casts **Chain Lightning** — the most damage the DM says he's ever seen it roll — hitting Scottishbard, Casimir and Phoenix Cloak. Casimir saves. Phoenix Cloak takes 55, tries Uncanny Dodge (doesn't apply — not an attack, and he can't see the caster), then remembers **Evasion** and takes 28 instead. **Scottishbard fails, burns his inspiration, fails anyway, and goes down.**
  - Parriwimple arrives at the window demanding "give us back dog, I want dog," casts **Power Word Poo** at the problem, and then spends most of the fight climbing, since he has no climb speed.
  - The DM asks the table's permission to be mean, gets it, and casts **Finger of Death** on Dog. Dog rolls a natural 20 on camera — physically holding the die up to his phone to prove it — for 29 necrotic instead of 57, and starts vomiting everywhere. He asks to puke on the guardian. Permitted, ineffective: "he's like, *you nasty*."
  - Under the hood is not a statue at all but some kind of hooded fox- or wolf-man in glasses, which the table immediately classifies as a furry ("I'm here too to kill furries" — Parriwimple). He announces himself: "**I'm the guardian of this temple, and you shall not pass me.**"
  - He **Dimensional Doors** across the room to escape the melee. Casimir answers with Cone of Cold and rolls terribly — and the guardian looks cold-resistant on top of it. Dablin's magic missile (4th level) lands, and his 3rd-level **Shatter** finally finds a hole: no thunder resistance, full damage. Between that and a dodged spell the party works out he probably has **advantage on saves against spells**.
  - He fireballs the group one more time. Phoenix Cloak passes for no damage; another player eats 25; the unconscious bard takes damage while down, which sparks a rules check on whether an AoE hit costs one death save or two. The DM pulls out the actual Player's Handbook rather than trusting a chatbot ("AI is not your friend right now") and rules **one**.
  - **Bulbin finishes it** — moves in, rages, throws his spear on the reasoning that the guardian would just teleport away again, hits with a 21, and kills him.
- Casimir stabilizes Scottishbard with a medicine check. Then Dog chews the plus-marked bone next to him and **the party heals 8 HP each**. (The DM says afterward he planted the bone deliberately: he doesn't like banishment, and wanted the banished player to have something to actually do.)
- **Looting the guardian.** He has a **spellbook**, and the DM reveals why the encounter was tuned so hard: the party gets access to **every spell he had** — chain lightning, finger of death, fireball, dimensional door, cone of cold, magic missile — even if Dablin can't cast the 5th-level ones yet. "This entire place loves mages." He's also carrying a **Robe of Useful Items**, patches and all, including mastiffs ("he has doggos"), a portable ram and a pit.
- In a further room the party turns up a very fancy magical staff. Bulbin hands it straight to Dablin — "wizards be eating good today, but barbarians be getting shit" — and Dablin identifies it as a **Staff of Frost**. Requires attunement, and Dablin can tell it was **very much cursed at some point**, though the curse is gone now. He also feels "a little funny" the moment he touches it, which the DM tells him to keep in mind for later.
- The party decides not to open the door with the six armed people behind it, plans a short rest for next time, and calls it there. DM: "Welcome to the Amber Temple."

## Key decisions
- Left **Irina** behind in the warm tower rather than take her into the temple, and left **Rudolph van Richten** with her as protection — knowingly giving up the party's only dedicated healer for the dungeon.
- Went in the front door instead of squeezing through the broken gap where the wolf was denned.
- Sent Dog through an arrow slit to retrieve the skeleton's wand, and spent 10 minutes ritual-casting Identify on it before moving on.
- Bulbin closed the door on six armed strangers instead of opening a second front mid-fight.
- Gave the Staff of Frost to Dablin rather than arguing over it.
- Stopped at the entrance hall rather than pushing deeper on low resources.

## Combat / danger
- **The temple guardian** (the hooded figure inside the eyeless statue) — a full spellcaster with Fireball, Chain Lightning, Finger of Death, Banishment, Dimensional Door and a stack of flaming skull minions in the arrow slits. Resistant to cold and piercing, **immune to nothing thunder-shaped**, and apparently has advantage on saves against spells. The skulls are immune to fire.
- **Scottishbard went down** to Chain Lightning and spent most of the fight unconscious, taking further death-save failures from a later fireball. Stabilized after the fight by Casimir, then healed by Dog's bone.
- **Dog was banished** mid-fight into a nightmare dimension and came back with an item, which is the best possible outcome for being removed from a combat.
- **Dog survived Finger of Death** on a natural 20 — 29 necrotic instead of 57.
- No deaths. The DM admits he genuinely expected someone to die: "if he had chain lightning and finger of death, it made no sense that someone would survive."

## Loot & discoveries
- **Wand of Secrets** — from the skeleton in the right-hand arrow-slit room; identified by Dablin.
- **The guardian's spellbook** — grants access to every spell he cast, including chain lightning, finger of death, fireball, dimensional door and cone of cold. Several are above Dablin's current level.
- **Robe of Useful Items** — patches include mastiffs, a portable ram and a pit.
- **Staff of Frost** — requires attunement, given to Dablin. Formerly cursed (the curse is gone), and it made Dablin feel "a little funny" on contact. Flagged by the DM as something to remember.
- **A bone marked with a plus sign** — looted by Dog from the nightmare dimension; chewing it healed the party 8 HP each.
- Lore: the Amber Temple "loves mages," per the DM. The dungeon is deliberately brutal, and there's a reason he can't explain yet.

## NPCs encountered
- **The temple guardian** *(killed)* — a hooded fox- or wolf-like figure in glasses, hidden inside a 40-foot eyeless statue behind magical darkness. Declared himself the guardian of the temple. Killed by Bulbin's thrown spear.
- **Casimir** — came along; offered the party the chance to stay behind, failed his Dispel Magic on the hood, cast a badly-rolled Cone of Cold, stabilized Scottishbard afterward, and sensibly backed off when he got low. "He's an actual person," per the DM.
- **Parriwimple** — climbed most of the fight, cast Power Word Poo, wanted Dog back.
- **Irina** — stayed behind at the tower rather than risk the temple.
- **Rudolph van Richten** — stayed behind to guard Irina and keep the fire going.
- **Six armed strangers** *(seen, not engaged)* — awake and sharpening weapons behind a second-floor door in the entrance hall. Bulbin shut the door before they noticed.
- **A wolf** *(seen, not engaged)* — denned in a side chamber reachable through a broken gap in the temple's outer wall.

## Fallen
No deaths this session — the closest calls were Scottishbard (down twice over) and Dog (Finger of Death).

## Cliffhanger / where we left off
The entrance hall of the Amber Temple is cleared and the guardian is dead. The party is standing in it, banged up and pre-short-rest, with a Staff of Frost, a Robe of Useful Items and a dead mage's spellbook. Unopened: the second-floor door with six armed people behind it, a hallway with a burnt corpse in it, another set of doors past that, and the wolf's den outside.
