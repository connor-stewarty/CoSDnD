# Session 01 — New Allies and the Amber Temple Lead

> **A note on numbering:** this is the first session we've actually *recorded and logged* — the campaign itself has been running about a year, so a lot of history predates this file (including whatever happened in "the house" mentioned below). Treat this archive's numbering as starting from here, not as the true beginning of the story. Once earlier sessions get written up from memory (if ever), this file's number may need to shift.

**Date played:** 2026-07-23
**Real-world session length:** ~67 minutes recorded (transcript starts mid-scene, so the full session likely ran longer than what got captured)
**In-game date:** Roughly one full evening through the following early afternoon (long rest starting ~6pm, travel through the night, a second long rest, waking ~6am, arriving back in town by early afternoon)

## TL;DR
Fresh out of a house where the party lost people to something ghoul-like (not true vampires, but nastily tanky — close to 200 HP and self-healing unless burned or hit with radiant), the party picked up two new allies — Dog and Phoenix Cloak — then traveled to a Vistani/Dusk Elf camp near Vallaki and recruited a third: Casimir, an old dusk elf wizard who says he knows where the Amber Temple is and what it has to do with Strahd's immortality.

## What happened
- **Before this recording (from Connor's prep notes):** the party left the house where they'd lost party members — including **Cassius**, a wizard, whose player (Todd) rolled up a brand new character — went back to Vallaki, picked up a new party member called "Dog" (see NPCs/party below — not an animal, a person who plays it like one), and met Todd's new character, Phoenix Cloak, in a tavern in Vallaki, mid-celebration of Strahd (Vallaki apparently throwing some kind of pro-Strahd festivities).
- Recording picks up with the party sizing up Phoenix Cloak's group — bloody, wounded, clearly just been in a fight ("vampires... not really vampires, more like some type of ghoul"). Dablin has a thousand-yard-stare moment, thinking about brothers lost in "the Great Gnome Wars."
- A perception check turns up that a gemstone Phoenix is carrying might be magical.
- Phoenix asks to join the party on the quest to kill Strahd; a very solemn "what color is your hair" / "red? then I trust you" exchange seals it.
- Party takes a long rest at the inn (Irina, an established ally there, checks in on how everyone's doing). Dog sleeps curled up on the floor of Bulbin's room, then gets invited onto the bed instead.
- Phoenix attunes to the gemstone overnight — it's a **Stone of Luck** (+1 to ability checks and saving throws while attuned). Phoenix's character name is revealed properly for the first time: **Phoenix Cloak**.
- Waking up (in-game ~6pm), the party debates next steps: they can't realistically fight more of the tanky ghoul-things right now, so the plan becomes "find resources and allies instead." Someone brings up a mysterious witch and asks the DM (William) about witch covens — reminding the table that ~2 in-game weeks ago they killed three hags in a windmill, and there's a superstition that killing witches in a coven weakens the survivors (unconfirmed "tall tale," like garlic and vampires). Recapping the fight that killed Cassius: fire and radiant damage were the only things that stopped the ghouls from regenerating — Cassius's fire, and radiant damage from someone else (transcribed as "Gerland" — possibly Scottishbard, unconfirmed).
- Ultimately the party decides to check out a nearby Vistani camp instead (about an hour's walk), partly to re-gear after losing items in the recent fight, partly on a tip that the Vistani (and the Dusk Elves camped alongside them) predate Strahd and might know useful lore. Bulbin also wants to talk to the innkeeper for local legends first.
- At the inn downstairs, an NPC (**Sushura**) points them toward the Vistani camp specifically — mentions "dusk scalps" and the chance to recruit a fighter, since the Dusk Elves loathe Strahd.
- Small comic-relief detour: Bulbin, mildly drunk, nearly combusts from both ends ("the double dragon") on the walk over.
- At the camp, the party first approaches the Dusk Elf guards rather than the Vistani. An old, nearly-blind dusk elf recounts the tragedy that shaped his people: Strahd was set to marry a dusk elf woman; she died (or was murdered) because she loved someone else, and Strahd, enraged, slaughtered the dusk elves — women and children included. Survivors now can't stray far from the Vistani camp or they "disappear."
- The old dusk elf points them to **Casimir**, described as the group's leader, living in the only open building nearby.
- Casimir welcomes them in — and right at that moment, Scottishbard introduces himself with what might be the character's whole personality in one line ("well, I'm Scottish, bard") before shaking Casimir's hand — the first handshake of the whole campaign, per the table. Casimir fills in more of Strahd's backstory: Strahd was originally human, has died and come back at least twice more since becoming a vampire, and anyone who's managed to kill him has also ended up dead. Casimir believes the secret to Strahd's immortality lies in the **Amber Temple**, built by an ancient society, historically guarded only by the (now presumably gone) Knights of the Silver Dragon as the last line between Strahd and the temple. Strahd got through anyway, and things escalated from there — including the dusk elf tragedy, which turns out to loop back to the same doomed-romance thread (Strahd's dead bride keeps reincarnating; he keeps falling for her).
- Casimir offers to join the party if they'll go to the Amber Temple with him — and reveals he actually knows where it is. He pulls up the map: it's out past **Barovia** village, on the way to a place the party very carefully avoids saying out loud (referred to at the table as "Babo Lasagna" — almost certainly a garbled stab at a real name; worth double-checking with your DM, but that general direction/location tends to ring a bell for this module).
- Casimir warns the temple is a point of no return, treacherous, and rigged with ancient magical traps — good news for the party, since it turns out Casimir himself is a wizard. He recommends gathering as many allies as possible first.
- Party agrees to swing through Barovia village on the way, both to look around and because they have an ally there. Route: Vallaki → Barovia village → (eventually) toward the Amber Temple.
- Travel montage: a full night on the road (7pm–10pm push, then camping in a previously-cleared area for safety — Scottishbard on door-barring duty again, naturally), two "encounters" that turned out to be nothing (a bundle of ordinary clothes; a broken gravestone that just says "UP DOG" — a joke, not a lead), rations in the morning, and finally arriving back at a familiar town by early-to-mid afternoon.
- Running gag: at some point Dog gets hit with an *enlarge* spell and becomes a giant version of himself, prompting a round of "Clifford the Big Red Dog" jokes that the table never quite lets go of.
- Setting up the map back in town, a leftover token accidentally duplicates — cue "the ghost of Cassius" jokes, landing harder than intended since Cassius is, in fact, actually dead.

## Key decisions
- Chose not to pursue another fight with the tanky ghoul-creatures for now — fire and radiant damage stopped their self-healing, but at ~200 HP each, tankiness alone made it not worth it yet.
- Chose to prioritize the Vistani/Dusk Elf camp over the witch lead, mainly for re-gearing and recruiting, since the witch angle felt riskier and less certain.
- Agreed to route through Barovia village before committing to the long, one-way trip to the Amber Temple, to pick up more allies first.

## Combat / danger
No combat during the recorded portion — this was a downtime, roleplay, and travel session. The dangerous fight (the one that cost "some good men") happened before the recording started; per Connor's prep notes, it's tied to leaving "the house where our party members died."

## Loot & discoveries
- **Stone of Luck** — found on/attuned to Phoenix Cloak; +1 to ability checks and saving throws while attuned.
- A lead on the **Amber Temple's** general location, past Barovia village.
- Strahd's tragic backstory (dead/unfaithful bride-to-be, reincarnation curse) and a working theory that the Amber Temple holds the secret to his immortality.
- A whole lot of nothing: a bundle that turned out to be spare clothes, and lint in the pocket ("we found a Catholic holiday").
