## TL;DR
A rolling fog swallowed the road behind us and spat us out somewhere that isn't on any map we know. A raven watched us from a dead tree the whole time.

## What happened
- Party regrouped after the [previous adventure hook] and got caught in an unnaturally thick mist on the road.
- The road behind vanished; walking "back" only led further into the fog.
- Emerged onto a muddy road flanked by twisted trees and a wrought-iron gate reading nothing we could read.
- Found a gravestone with a fresh, still-legible name none of us recognized.
- A pale figure watched us from a hillside distance, then was gone.

## Key decisions
Decided to follow the only road rather than strike out cross-country — visibility was too low and something was clearly herding us toward it.

## Combat / danger
None yet — but everyone rolled a passive Perception check and something in the trees was definitely rolling back.
