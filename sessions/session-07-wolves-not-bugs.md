# Session 07 — Wolves, Not Bugs

**Date played:** 2026-11-19
**Real-world session length:** ~150 minutes recorded (~2h30m)
**In-game date:** Leaves the Amber Temple around 5pm, back to the tower that night for the first long rest in a long while, then a full day's travel — arriving at the witch's village around 5pm the next day.

## TL;DR
The party walked out of the Amber Temple, talked their way past one ambush and ate another, and got back to the tower for the first proper sleep in days. Rudolph van Richten cast Detect Good and Evil over them, found them reading as evil, and explained what that cost: Strahd no longer thinks of them as bugs. They pressed on to the witch's village — finally putting a name to Session 01's "Babo Lasagna" — met the ghost who started the whole tragedy, learned Irina is the reincarnation of Strahd's first love, and ended the night touching a statue they already knew was trapped.

## What happened
- Out of the temple at about 5pm, with the DM's tally: "**made it out of the Amber Temple, only one death, and that was the guy who got too hungry for power.**" (Dog has a replacement character ready, just in case.)
- No long rest is possible out here either. A perception check (24) spells it out: the fur and pelts work while you're moving and for an hour's break, but **eight hours asleep in this cold would kill you.** The only option is to keep walking.
- **The bandit bridge.** Thugs on the far side demand money to pass, then double it to 200 gold. Scottishbard walks up harmless, passes a deception check, and casts **Suggestion** — they've already been paid, let these people through. The other bandits fail to notice a spell was cast at all. The party walks across without a fight. "**Bard is carrying us.**"
  - Phoenix Cloak hides before crossing anyway, throwing snow over his cloak to blend in (stealth 22). The DM notes that if he'd failed, hiding right before crossing a bridge would have looked extremely suspicious.
- **The goat.** A giant goat ambushes the party on a ledge — a scripted encounter whose entire purpose, the DM explains afterwards, is to **ram someone a hundred feet off the mountain**. Phoenix Cloak sneak-attacks it, Dog opportunity-attacks it as it tries to reposition, takes its head off and starts eating. Parriwimple hollows out the skull and wears it as a hat, so he is now a flying man with bone wings and a goat skull for a helmet. "How do I look?"
- **Back at the tower.** Irina and Rudolph van Richten are still there with the fire going. Van Richten takes one look at Parriwimple's wings and the rest of them and asks for a moment — then quietly casts **Detect Good and Evil** over the party.
  - The verdict: "**you guys were fine before you left, but now it's like you're completely different people.**" He says plainly that the reading came back **evil** for some of them.
  - The rundown of what he's looking at: Parriwimple with bone wings, Scottishbard with an extra finger on each hand, Bulbin enormous, Dog enormous and with no eyes at all, Dablin unchanged, and Phoenix Cloak — who took nothing — exactly as he was. "He's still a good boy."
- **Everyone finally long rests.** Full HP, all hit dice back, rages back, one level of exhaustion cleared.
- **Van Richten's morning briefing**, which is the heart of the session. He asks what the party is willing to do to kill Strahd, then lays out what the gifts have changed:
  - "**Strahd no longer sees you as bugs. He probably sees you as wolves now** — at least, ones that aren't a part of his territory. And he will try to kill you." The party has gone from beneath notice to roughly top priority.
  - **Strahd can scry them.** He sees most of this land, and if any of his people ever took something of yours, he can use it to find your exact location.
  - Pressed on it, the DM points at **Bulbin**: about two years ago, while he was asleep and extremely drunk in the Vistani camp, they took **a torn piece of his clothing and some gold**. He never noticed. That is a live scrying link, on the only original party member still alive besides Scottishbard.
- Plans discussed: re-obtaining **two relics** — a sword and an amulet, previously carried by Cassius and another fallen ally. Problem: the sword requires a **good alignment** to use, and after the Amber Temple the party is, by their own estimate, "probably neutral and not good aligned." They may no longer be able to wield their own best weapons against Strahd.
- **The Tome of Understanding** finally comes up. It's +2 Wisdom for 48 hours of study, and nobody wants it: Scottishbard is a charisma caster, Dog points out he has no eyes to be charmed through, and giving it to Parriwimple is floated mostly as a joke before someone notes he'd then be a better target for Strahd to charm and turn. **Kept in reserve** in case someone dies and a new character needs it.
- **Back on the road.** A giant bird attacks from 60 feet up. It opens by casting **Sanctuary on itself** so it can't be attacked while it doesn't attack, which it immediately abandons.
  - Parriwimple offers rides. Dog takes one, gets **dropped onto the bird from above** (acrobatics 14 against the bird's dex save — it rolled a natural 1 and a natural 20, and the 1 is what counted), and spends the fight clinging to it.
  - The bird grabs Dog in its talons. Phoenix Cloak lands a natural 20 from range for 45, Bulbin throws a javelin for 11 and loses the javelin, Dablin firebolts and magic-missiles, Scottishbard's Dissonant Whispers sends it 120 feet across the map.
  - Eventually it panics, **drops Dog and flees**. Dog fails the save to catch himself, falls — and Parriwimple flies in and catches him, reducing it to 7 bludgeoning. Afterwards Dog is "trembling and silent, staring off into the abyss."
- **The druids, again.** A group the party has fought before steps out of the trees: "we knew you would be here." A persuasion check (13) gets a **mixed success** — one druid looks at Bulbin's new physique, says "your muscles are super swole, man, I'll meet you back on Tuesday," and walks off. The rest shout "traitor!" and fight anyway.
  - Mid-fight, **tentacled monstrosities** uncloak from the rocks in a surprise round and start grappling and biting people. Phoenix Cloak uses Uncanny Dodge to halve one.
  - The party wins it. Loot: about 20 gold — and Dog **harvests the calamari**, which Dablin works out has enough residual magic in it to render down into a **grease spell in a bottle**. The stated plan: make Strahd slip on his own feet.
- **The witch's village.** The party arrives around 5pm at a place they'd only ever heard was home to a witch. A **were-raven** meets them and tells them to leave:
  - This is **Baba Lysaga's** village — which the DM pronounces properly once and then cheerfully calls "**Baba Lasagna**" for the rest of the night because it's funnier. **This is Session 01's "Babo Lasagna," finally identified.**
  - It used to be thriving. Strahd had his way with it after someone he loved died here, and killed everyone in it.
  - Baba Lysaga is "the most powerful wizard of this land" and "might be able to beat Strahd in a sheer magic battle alone."
  - The raven offers one question before leaving. The party asks the meaning of life. The answer is lost to a mic cutting out.
- Across a warm, slow river (Bulbin, a turtle, swims it comfortably; Parriwimple flies, slowly) the party finds a **stone circle**: nine frightened goats that won't leave the ring, fenced by posts topped with **fifty human skulls**, ten feet apart. Nobody can speak goat. Dablin tries Infernal. The goats are just scared.
- Searching the ruins kicks off a brief fight with **four venomous snakes** hidden in the bushes — notable mainly for how hard they hit (one rolled maximum poison damage) and for Dablin's stout halfling advantage against poison.
- **The ghost.** In the ruins of a mansion stands the ghost of a giant man, features mutilated, entrails hanging loose. He tells them to leave his house, then explains himself:
  - "**I was the one who tried to stop Strahd in this village, and I paid the price.**"
  - "**I killed Strahd's lover, Maria, before she could become a vampire — and thus doomed her soul. All the village burned because of it.**" He thought he was doing good. His whole village died for it.
  - He can't pass on, can't leave, can't even wander — he's stationary, can only manifest strongly when people are near, and believes he could only possess a humanoid.
  - Asked what he knows of Strahd: "**he's kind of a simp.** He's very fixated on his goals... once we killed her, he didn't want to do anything besides punish us until we were a bunch of rubble."
  - Van Richten suggests the thing anchoring him is probably **a physical object**, not an emotional one, since he seems broadly at peace with what he did.
- **The statue.** A perception check (27) turns up a ten-foot statue in the likeness of a peasant girl holding a rose — and a wisdom check lands the realization that **it is Irina's face**.
  - Van Richten puts it together: Irina is "**most likely the next reincarnation of Strahd's first love.**" His explanation of how: when a soul leaves this realm, the fog doesn't let it go, so it simply goes into a new body. Irina has been trapped here as long as Strahd has.
  - The party confirms the statue is **magical** (arcana 15) and **trapped** — the **Wand of Secrets** lights it up like a BG3 trap outline. They discuss it, back up a few hundred steps, and have Irina touch it with a **mage hand** anyway.
- **Cliffhanger.** The statue glows purple, and things start crawling out of the graves all around it. Not the flaming skulls, to everyone's audible relief — **zombies and more snakes**. That's where the night ends, mid-initiative.

## Key decisions
- Talked past the bandit bridge with Suggestion instead of fighting — the party was in no state for an encounter and knew it.
- Left the Amber Temple behind rather than go back for the rest of the dark gifts or the golem-guarded hoard.
- **Kept the Tome of Understanding unused**, deliberately held for whoever needs it most later — possibly a replacement character.
- Did not give it to Parriwimple, on the grounds that a wiser NPC is also a more valuable one for Strahd to charm and turn against them.
- Touched the statue despite confirming it was both magical and trapped.

## Combat / danger
- **The goat** — a scripted ledge encounter built to knock someone a hundred feet off a mountain. Killed before it got the chance.
- **The giant bird** — opened with Sanctuary, grappled Dog in its talons sixty feet up, and fled when it got low. Dog was dropped and caught by Parriwimple.
- **The druids and the tentacled things** — a fight the party partly talked its way out of (one druid defected on the spot) before ambushers uncloaked from the rocks.
- **Snakes** in the bushes at the village — small, and hitting far above their weight on poison damage.
- **Standing threat, new this session:** Strahd is actively hunting them now, and can scry Bulbin through something stolen two years ago.

## Loot & discoveries
- **A bottled grease spell**, rendered by Dablin out of harvested calamari.
- About 20 gold off the druids. A goat-skull hat for Parriwimple. One javelin lost.
- **Baba Lysaga's village identified** — Session 01's "Babo Lasagna." Strahd destroyed it after a woman he loved died here.
- **Irina is the reincarnation of Strahd's first love.** Souls can't leave Barovia; the fog turns them back into new bodies.
- **The party now reads as evil** under Detect Good and Evil, and Strahd has reclassified them from beneath notice to a genuine threat.
- **Strahd can scry Bulbin** via a torn piece of clothing taken by the Vistani about two years ago.
- The **Sunsword and amulet** may be unusable by the party now, because the sword requires a good alignment.
- The **Tome of Understanding** is still unspent (+2 Wisdom, 48 hours of study).

## NPCs encountered
- **Rudolph van Richten** — cast Detect Good and Evil on the party, told them what they'd become, and explained Strahd's new interest in them. Also healing people for 11-12 off first-level slots all session. The closest thing the party has to a conscience now.
- **Irina** — revealed as the reincarnation of Strahd's first love, with a ten-foot statue of her face standing in a ruined village. Touched the statue with a mage hand at the party's request.
- **The ghost of the village** *(not hostile)* — a huge, mutilated figure who killed Strahd's lover Maria to stop her becoming a vampire, and whose village burned for it. Cannot leave or pass on. Thinks something physical is anchoring him.
- **The were-raven** — warned the party off, named Baba Lysaga and her village, and flew away.
- **Baba Lysaga** *(not yet met)* — "the most powerful wizard of this land," possibly a match for Strahd in a straight magic duel. Her house is some 700 feet off, across the ruins.
- **The druid who defected** — walked off mid-standoff because Bulbin looked too strong to fight. Called a traitor by his own side.
- **Parriwimple** — flew, caught Dog mid-fall, and made a hat out of a goat.

## DM notes / questions for next time
- **Resolved from Session 01:** "Babo Lasagna" is **Baba Lysaga**, and the village is hers. The DM says the funnier pronunciation is staying.
- **What anchors the ghost?** Van Richten thinks a physical object. The statue of Irina was the obvious candidate and turned out to be a trap — worth asking whether it's also the anchor.
- **The scrying problem.** Bulbin is findable through a piece of clothing the Vistani took two years ago. Is it recoverable, and does destroying it help?
- **Can the party still use the Sunsword?** It needs a good alignment, and van Richten's spell says they aren't. Worth asking William directly before planning around it.
- Does **Irina** know what she is now, and does being near Strahd's statue change anything for her?
- Confirm the spelling of **Maria** — the ghost said it once and the tape is not clear.
- The Tome of Understanding is still on the shelf. Decide who gets it before someone dies rather than after.
