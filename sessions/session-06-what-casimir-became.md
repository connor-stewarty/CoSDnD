# Session 06 — What Casimir Became

**Date played:** 2026-11-05
**Real-world session length:** ~171 minutes recorded (~2h51m)
**In-game date:** Straight on from Session 05 — the same hour, working through the Amber Temple's lower level room by room.

## TL;DR
Casimir kept taking dark gifts until there was nothing left of him worth saving, announced he intended to rule Barovia once Strahd was dead, and the party killed him. His ghost jumped into an amber guardian and they killed that too. Everyone except Phoenix Cloak took at least one gift of their own, a room of fireball-slinging skulls then put most of the party on the floor, and they agreed to leave the temple and come back at full strength.

## What happened
- Picks up mid-search. Casimir asks what the two remaining gifts in the last room showed, hears about the scroll one, and says the quiet part out loud: "**if we get enough power here we could probably kill Strahd. We just keep taking power.**" Then he touches it.
  - It gives him **Scrying**. The change in him is immediate and physical: his voice drops to a lower rasp, and his smile now permanently shows all his teeth, cartoonishly wide.
  - The party pushes back. Didn't resurrecting your sister age you? "Well, yeah, but I don't actually feel old." What if it shortened your life? "**Then why not take even more power while I'm still alive?**" Told he's killing himself: "I'm not killing myself. I'm just ensuring that we can defeat Strahd."
- An arcane-locked door. The lich's name doesn't work this time, Parriwimple can't break it ("this is strong door!"), and **Phoenix Cloak picks it with thieves' tools** — the start of a running job for him this session.
- **A new vault**: amber-glazed walls shot through with red veins, three more sarcophagi. Something **invisible** ambushes Bulbin as he walks in. The DM quietly lets him rage before rolling initiative rather than catching him flat — "I'm playing this encounter a little bit nicer than it should be already."
  - The attacker is a small goblin-like thing that stabs for necrotic and spends a turn visibly **charging something yellow** in one hand.
  - It's **Cloudkill**. Scottishbard counterspells it successfully, which triggers a full DM rant: "**I hate counterspell. It's the stupidest spell in the game**... it just says, hey, I'm gonna use my thing to say you can't use your thing." The table debates the 2024 rules change for a while.
  - Phoenix Cloak finishes it by cutting its head off. The DM, disappointed on its behalf: "I thought he was going to be a lot stronger than he was. He looked very intimidating on my sheet."
- **The gifts, taken in earnest.** The DM sends each one's actual effect privately, so the table only hears the vision and whatever the player chooses to say out loud:
  - **Bulbin** takes the one showing a man holding the world on his shoulders. His muscles visibly grow; he reports feeling "**as strong as a fire giant**," and no downside at all. He's given a flaw in private.
  - **Dog** takes several: one showing a gladiator summoning two wolves to harry his opponent ("that fits dogs so well" — Dog howls on accepting), "the big muscle" one, and one that costs him his **remaining eye**. He already wore a patch over the left; now both sockets are empty, and he can still see perfectly. The party offers glass eyes. He'd rather wear two patches.
  - **Scottishbard** takes one showing a man yapping while others act out what he says — steal, kill, fight. He grows a **sixth finger on each hand** ("six-fingered crow, I guess?") and picks up a flaw the DM works out with him privately: greedy, and wanting the centre of attention.
  - **Dablin** takes one with no visible change at all.
  - **Parriwimple** takes the one promising the power of the grave wyrm, and **bone wings erupt from his back**. Fly speed around 50. The entire table immediately asks whether he can now Power Word Poo on people from above. He can.
  - **Phoenix Cloak takes nothing**, all session.
  - Declined by everyone: a gift of healing in sunlight (the party has no sunlight source since their cleric left), one of spreading disease, one of lightning, one of raising the recently dead as zombies, and one of shielding your mind.
- Meanwhile **Casimir touches every sarcophagus in reach and takes all of them.** He comes back enormous, eyes glowing, and talks like a different person:
  - "Once Strahd's dead, we could all rule the land together... **Of course we can't fully let the people decide who wants to rule, because they're just gonna choose another Strahd. So that's why we should be the ones who rule over them.**"
  - Out of character, immediately: "**Guys, I think we need to kill this guy.**"
  - He suplexes a poltergeist into the floor hard enough to crack it, tells it "that's right, I want to hear you beg," then grabs Parriwimple's arm when Parriwimple lands the kill: "**I was supposed to kill him. Not you.**" Shoved aside with "whatever, weakling."
  - Challenged over it, with a spell visibly building in his hand: "**I've realized what true strength is. And y'all can either choose to live in the past, or join me.**" Answered with "true strength are the friends we made along the way." He casts **Greater Invisibility** and leaves.
- A quieter stretch: a wizard's chamber, a broken workshop, a **spell scroll of Wall of Fire** found and handed to Dablin, and more arcane-locked doors — Phoenix Cloak picking what he can, Bulbin and Parriwimple breaking what he can't.
- **The fight with Casimir.** He comes back. He counterspells Scottishbard's counterspell ("**get rekt, freaking nerd!**" — the DM has clearly enjoyed waiting for this), drops a 36-damage area spell that Phoenix Cloak walks out of untouched, and goes down to **Bulbin's crit**.
  - Dying, he screams "**I won't die**" — and his ghost rises, absorbs a ring off his own body, and flies into the amber guardian standing nearby, which turns red and keeps fighting. Its armour re-materializes as it's chipped away.
  - Phoenix Cloak ends that too, poking it through its remaining eye: "not so strong now, are you?"
  - Dog eats part of Casimir's body, then urinates on it. Nobody objects. "At the end, it was deserved."
- **Loot from Casimir:** his **spell book** (counterspell and fly among them) to Dablin, and a **Ring of Warmth** to Phoenix Cloak — finally some cold protection for someone who had only a blanket. In the same conversation Phoenix Cloak discovers he's been carrying the **Stone of Good Luck** since Session 01 without ever writing it down: +1 to all ability checks and saving throws.
- **The skull room.** Opening the last door lets out three flaming skulls that are **immune to fire** and throw Fireball after Fireball, including into their own room. It very nearly ends the campaign:
  - Scottishbard's **Aid**, cast earlier, is the only reason it doesn't — repeatedly called "so clutch" afterwards.
  - **Bulbin, Dog and Parriwimple all go down.** Phoenix Cloak takes zero damage from every single fireball, because **Evasion** plus a +7 Dex save means he effectively cannot fail. "This whole class is meant to counter fireballs."
  - Dog is stabilized by a medicine check; Dablin finishes the last skulls with magic missile.
- Afterwards: "**I don't know how much more we can take of this. We may need to start leaving.**" With no rages, almost no spell slots and three people on the floor, the party agrees to cut their losses.
- They **build a fire** out of the broken beds and bookshelves to get through the hour it takes someone to wake up — a survival check of 23, and the DM rules it a one-time trick, because the wood is now gone.
- One last test of the hoard: Dog deliberately rattles a pile of electrum with mage hand to see what happens. The **amber golem** drops invisibility, walks over, and smashes the pile flat. They leave it all behind again. "Maybe we could come back later."
- They head for the stairs out, past Exethanter (no thanks, we're going), with one more cold save on the way. Everyone passes. Next session starts with the walk back to the tower — or with the party trying to work out how to long rest at well below freezing.
- **The DM's one admission, at the end:** "The only thing I edited was **Kezmar wasn't necessarily supposed to become evil**, but storyline-wise it made sense... **some of the failed rolls he had, because he has to make the same rolls you guys do.** So it made sense that he just became evil after all of his failed rolls. It's like, how do I show that he's evil? Oh, what if I just have him keep on taking powers."

## Key decisions
- **Killed Casimir.** A party member since Session 01, the man who led them to the Amber Temple in the first place.
- Every player character except Phoenix Cloak accepted at least one dark gift, having watched exactly what the gifts did to Casimir.
- Declined the gifts of disease, lightning, raising the dead, sunlight-healing and mind-shielding.
- Left the hoard untouched a second time, after deliberately provoking the golem to confirm it still guards it.
- Burned the room's furniture for one fire rather than press on cold — knowingly spending the only wood down there.
- **Agreed to leave the Amber Temple** and come back at full strength rather than open another door.

## Combat / danger
- **The invisible ambusher** in the second vault — necrotic stabs and a charged Cloudkill, counterspelled before it landed.
- **Casimir** — counterspelled the party's counterspell, hit for 36 in an area, and died to a Bulbin crit. Then his ghost took over the amber guardian, which re-grew its armour as it was damaged, and had to be killed separately.
- **The flaming skulls** — fire-immune, Fireball-spamming, and the closest this campaign has come to a wipe. Bulbin, Dog and Parriwimple all went down. Phoenix Cloak took zero damage across the entire fight.
- Standing conditions: no long rests in the temple, no rages left, exhaustion accumulating from the cold, and most of the party out of resources.

- **Ring of Warmth** — off Casimir's body, to Phoenix Cloak. Cold protection at last.
- **Spell scroll of Wall of Fire** — found in a side room, given to Dablin.
- **Stone of Good Luck** — not new, but newly written down: Phoenix Cloak has had it since Session 01. +1 to all ability checks and saving throws.

The DM sent each effect privately, so the party as a whole knows only the visions and what each person volunteered.
- Confirmed: **the gifts have flaws attached**, handed out individually and in private.

## Fallen
- **Casimir** — dusk elf wizard, leader of the dusk elves, party member since Session 01. Killed by Bulbin in the Amber Temple's lower level after taking every dark gift he could reach. Died saying "I won't die," then possessed a suit of armour and died again. Dog ate part of him.

## Cliffhanger / where we left off
On the way out of the Amber Temple, battered, out of rages and spell slots, carrying a spell book, a ring, a scroll and a set of dark gifts nobody fully understands yet. Casimir is dead by the party's hand. The hoard is still guarded. Next session is the walk back to the tower where Irina and Rudolph van Richten are waiting — or the attempt to work out how to long rest in a place that sits well below freezing.

## DM notes / questions for next time
- **What do the gifts actually do?** Each was sent privately, so the party collectively doesn't know what anyone else got. Worth deciding how much the characters tell each other — especially given everyone just watched where that road ends.
- **What are the flaws?** Bulbin and Scottishbard both have one. Scottishbard's is greed and wanting the centre of attention. Dog's and Dablin's, if any, weren't said aloud.
- Dog's gift cost him his remaining eye and he can still see. Worth pinning down what he's seeing with.
- **Does Casimir stay dead?** His ghost moved into a suit of armour once already. Also: his resurrected sister is still out there somewhere, and nobody has told her.
- The party has a **single-use Resurrection** (Scottishbard) and now a dead ally. Nobody raised it at the table. Also still unresolved: using it on Cassius, which needs a body.
- Who leads now? Casimir was the one who knew where the Amber Temple was and what it meant.
- **How does anyone long rest here?** The fire trick is spent, the wood is gone, and the DM flagged Tiny Hut as the obvious answer while making clear how he feels about it.
- The hoard is confirmed still golem-guarded. The DM let slip that **one of the treasures the party needs for Strahd may be in one of those piles** — he said it wasn't, but said it in a way worth remembering.
- Still open: Parriwimple's spelling, whether Esmeralda is Session 01's "Gerland," and the "Garland" who'd have loved the holy symbols.
