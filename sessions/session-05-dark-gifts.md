# Session 05 — Dark Gifts

**Date played:** 2026-10-15
**Real-world session length:** ~148 minutes recorded (~2h28m)
**In-game date:** Straight on from Session 04 — the lich's library, the stairs down, and the sarcophagus vault beneath it. (About five real-world weeks passed between sessions; the DM opened with "as if a month didn't pass.")

## TL;DR
The party got the lich's blessing to go downstairs, learned his name, and found the amber sarcophagi — which offer real power at a cost the temple does not explain up front. Casimir took one, turned corpse-grey, and used it to resurrect the sister he murdered to stop her becoming Strahd's bride; she woke up, heard that, and cast Time Stop. Then they met Rahadin, Strahd's number-one servant and the man who sold out Casimir's people, and chose not to fight him.

## What happened
- **The lich, continued.** Asked straight out whether they'd come seeking power, he says there's "a thing of immense power" under the library, that it comes at great cost, and that as "the true keeper of this place" they have his permission to go — but he can't guarantee anything down there won't attack them. There are old security measures; he doesn't remember. "I have Alzheimer's... I don't even really remember my name anymore."
  - Pressed on what the cost is: "you'll know the cost when you're there." Then, flatly: "**I took one of the gifts. And look how I turned out.**"
  - He confirms there's a point of no return, but says it'll be obvious, and it isn't the stairs. The table translates: "we'll get a UI prompt that says, hey, are you sure you want to continue. Terms of service."
- A **persuasion check** on the way out gets the party a parting gift: the lich looks at Bulbin's battle axe, says "hocus pocus," waves a hand, and makes it **+1**. Dablin Horkus Porkus takes this personally — "I didn't know your name was magical."
- The lich then excuses himself: it's **bingo night**. He walks to his desk and minor-illusions a bingo board. Asked who calls the numbers when he's alone: "the voices."
- **His spell book.** An investigation check (22) spots an obviously, enormously magical book in his study that he isn't carrying. Asked about it, he says he doesn't remember it, can't read it anymore — "but if someone were to grab it, I would probably try to murder them." He opens it briefly to show them, and everyone makes a Constitution save; anyone under 15 **throws up on the spot**, like being blasted with radiation. The DM is upfront that this one is a meme — it costs nothing, it's there to show the power difference. It's written in **Abyssal**.
- **The library.** Most of the books look fine and crumble to dust the moment they're touched. One has a visible magical aura. Dablin checks it for traps with Casimir's help (arcana, 24), touches it, and absorbs it — all the letters going in through his eyes, ears, nose and mouth. He now **comprehends all languages, permanently**. The table's reference points: an enchanted book from Minecraft, the Dragonborn eating a dragon soul.
- With that, he can read the spine of the lich's own book, and the party finally gets a name: **Exethanter**.
- **Down the stairs.** Three **amber sarcophagi**, visibly radiating magic. The party is still chatting about whether proximity triggers anything when the boxes start rustling — the DM flags it as a callback to an old session — and **vampire spawn** climb out: "**We won't let you have this power.**"
- **The sarcophagus fight.** Nobody is surprised; as the DM puts it, "you guys saw boxes, and were like, huh, I wonder if these boxes are gonna have bad guys."
  - These are the regenerating things from "the house" all over again. They're **resistant to non-magical bludgeoning, piercing and slashing**, and they **heal every round** unless hit with radiant damage or holy water. Dog's non-magical battle axe does half damage; he drops it mid-fight, rages, and switches to his **magical claws**.
  - Bulbin's brand-new **+1 great axe** is magic, so it bypasses the resistance — the lich's gift pays for itself within five minutes.
  - **Dablin's Chill Touch** turns out to be the key: it stops them regaining hit points at all, and gives them disadvantage attacking him since they're undead. He spends most of the fight re-applying it.
  - Scottishbard uses **Dissonant Whispers** to force one to flee through the whole party, handing out four-plus opportunity attacks, and **Cutting Words** to shave damage off hits on Dog and Dablin.
  - Casimir drops a third-level Fireball (Dog has advantage on the save, since he can see it coming), Misty Steps out of trouble, and gets focused down for it.
  - Parriwimple **Action Surges**, does 32 damage, and gets nothing for it because he has no magic weapon.
  - Phoenix Cloak re-learns that **sneak attack doesn't require stealth** — advantage from flanking is enough — and that cunning action can be used to disengage. Then the table discovers his **rapier has been misconfigured the whole time**: a flat "2 + 6" instead of 2 + 1d8, which is why he kept doing exactly 8 damage every single hit. Fixed mid-combat.
  - **Long rests are impossible in here.** Confirmed on the spot: it's below freezing, so even with cold gear nobody can sleep a full night. Both barbarians are down to **one rage each** for the rest of the dungeon.
- The party wins it, Phoenix Cloak finishing the last spawn with a vertical slice from the head down.
- **The gifts.** The moment it's clear, **Casimir walks straight to a sarcophagus and touches it** — visibly out of character for him. His skin goes wrinkly and corpse-like. He says "I've done it. I got it," and immediately casts a spell he did not have before: an arcana check (natural 20, 28) identifies it as **Resurrection**.
  - What he resurrects is a **female dusk elf** — which the party clocks instantly as impossible, "like a legendary Pokémon." It's **his sister**. The table's working theory for how it was even castable: he's been carrying one of her fingers.
  - She wakes up barely conscious, and Casimir leads with: "You were going to become Strahd's bride, and I couldn't have that... **I did in fact murder you. But you're back now.**" She says "What?" three times and **casts Time Stop**. She is gone.
  - Casimir, afterward: "maybe I should have talked to you guys, that might not have been the smartest thing to say in the beginning." He admits the resurrection was the real reason he came to the Amber Temple, and apologizes for not saying so. He does not appear to notice he now looks like a corpse.
- **The three sarcophagi in this room**, touched one at a time (touching shows you a vision and a yes/no prompt; you can decline):
  - **Dark Gift of the Vampire** — a world of blood, a cave of teeth. Declined, on the reasonable theory that it's how Strahd made himself.
  - **Gift of Tenebrous** — books with energy running through them, a plant growing on one side and a pile of bodies on the other. Life and death. Looked at, not taken.
  - **The resurrection gift**, from someone the vision names only as **the Corpse Star** — a hand raising the dead. A **one-time** Resurrection, at the price of a **−4 penalty to everything until you take a long rest** — which, in this dungeon, you cannot. Casimir used his and lost the power. **Phoenix Cloak took it too**, and is now wrinkled, corpse-skinned, missing a good deal of his feathers, and carrying one resurrection and a −4 to everything.
  - The party immediately starts asking whether they could bring **Cassius** back. They'd need the body.
- Exethanter confirms there are **more of these throughout the temple**, and apologizes for not mentioning it — he was waiting to see whether the party was chill.
- **A short rest**, with Song of Rest, and Constitution saves against the cold for anyone without proper gear. Dog reports having been in single digits from full health during the fight.
- **The hoard.** Through a gap, piles of treasure: 17,000 copper, 12,000 silver, electrum, 30 gems, jewels, iron ingots, silvered weapons, rusty plate, **six fine dresses**, **30 holy symbols**, a gilded skull, eight warhammers, six war picks, an obsidian scepter, a silvered rapier, and a **gilded chariot**.
  - Dog walks face-first into something invisible in the middle of it. It's cold, metallic, doesn't echo. Investigation suggests a pillar. **See Invisibility** reveals the truth: a **giant golem made entirely of amber**, standing guard.
  - They take **nothing**. Not one coin.
  - The Wand of Secrets did flag a nearby door rather than the golem — the DM's reasoning being that a golem's existence isn't a trap, and pointing at it would have given the game away.
- **The arcane-locked door.** Rather than knock, lockpick or smash, Scottishbard tries **saying the lich's name at it**. It opens. He gets inspiration for solving a puzzle with no information whatsoever.
- **Rahadin.** In the next hall, a figure praying to one of the eyeless statues, mid-ritual, eating a live rat. Casimir takes one look and spits "**betrayer**." The man introduces himself: **Rahadin**, first servant of Strahd, part dusk elf and something like a vampire — and the one who **ratted out the dusk elves and had them massacred**, which is how he got his position.
  - He already knows the party is trying to kill Strahd: "that's pretty well known inside our networks." There are a lot of bats around; information moves easily.
  - His offer is simple: "**if y'all don't fight me I won't fight you. But if you do fight me I will murder all of you.**" He isn't worried either way, because Strahd is going to kill them anyway.
  - An insight check (22) with help reads him as genuinely dangerous: beatable at full strength, but not now, and probably not without losing someone. The party talks through luring him into the amber golem, decides it won't work, and **lets him walk out**.
- Further on: a second golem room where the amber golem has **already been destroyed** by someone, long ago. Nobody knows who.
- **The nothics.** Two hunched creatures in another chamber — identified on an arcana check (26) as **cursed wizards**, reduced to this by something they did, drawn to places thick with magic, and not automatically hostile. These two are friendly: "oh, you've seen the power too. You want it as well?" They tell the party to go ahead and touch the amber.
  - Two more gifts seen here: one showing a **freezing tundra with a fire burning impossibly in the cold** — "of ice and of hate" — and the **Gift of Secrets**, a room of scrolls with a magnifying glass that lets you understand things you couldn't see before. Dablin says he'll keep that one in mind. Neither taken.
- Session ends there, with the party deciding what to take and what it will cost them.

## Key decisions
- Went down the stairs on the lich's permission, knowing the power costs something and that nobody would say what.
- **Phoenix Cloak accepted the Corpse Star's gift** — a single resurrection for a −4 penalty to everything that only a long rest clears, in a dungeon where long rests are impossible.
- **Declined the Dark Gift of the Vampire** outright, on the theory that it's what Strahd used.
- Took **nothing** from an enormous treasure hoard rather than wake the amber golem guarding it.
- **Did not fight Rahadin.** Insight said they'd probably win eventually and probably lose someone doing it, and he's not Strahd.
- Left the nothics alone and let them be friendly.

## Combat / danger
- **Vampire spawn at the sarcophagi** — the regenerating things from "the house," again. Resistant to non-magical weapons, healing every round unless hit with radiant damage or holy water. Beaten by a combination of Dablin's Chill Touch shutting off regeneration, Bulbin's newly-enchanted axe, and Dog's claws. Dog went from full health to single digits.
- **Standing conditions:** no long rests anywhere in the temple, both barbarians on their last rage, the cold clock still running, and Phoenix Cloak now at **−4 to everything** with no way to clear it.
- **Rahadin** — not fought. Sized up as a fight the party could lose someone to right now.
- **The amber golem** — not fought, and only noticed because Dog walked into it.

## Loot & discoveries
- **Permanent comprehension of all languages** — Dablin, from the aura'd book in the library. Used immediately to read the lich's name.
- **The lich's name: Exethanter.** Also the password to at least one arcane-locked door in this place.
- **Bulbin's great axe is now +1**, courtesy of the lich.
- **One single-use Resurrection** — Phoenix Cloak, at the cost of a −4 penalty to everything until a long rest.
- **Gifts seen and declined so far:** Dark Gift of the Vampire, Gift of Tenebrous, a gift "of ice and of hate," and the Gift of Secrets. Exethanter says there are more scattered through the temple.
- **A hoard left untouched** behind an invisible amber golem — tens of thousands of coins, 30 holy symbols, six dresses, a gilded skull, an obsidian scepter and a gilded chariot among it.
- Confirmed mechanically: the vampire spawn **regenerate unless hit with radiant damage or holy water**, which is the same rule as the things that killed Cassius.

## NPCs encountered
- **Exethanter** *(the lich, now named)* — gave permission to go below, warned of a cost he couldn't describe, upgraded Bulbin's axe for a good persuasion roll, and went off to play bingo alone. Took one of the gifts himself, long ago — "and look how I turned out." His spell book is in Abyssal and makes people vomit; he'd kill to keep it.
- **Rahadin** *(new — hostile, not fought)* — Strahd's number-one servant. Dusk elf, something vampiric, eats live rats, prays to the temple's statues. **Personally responsible for selling out the dusk elves and having them massacred.** Casimir called him a betrayer to his face. Offered a truce as long as the party doesn't start anything.
- **Casimir's sister** *(resurrected, immediately gone)* — a female dusk elf, which the party was told shouldn't exist. Casimir murdered her to stop her becoming Strahd's bride, and brought her back with the Corpse Star's gift. On being told all of that, she cast Time Stop and vanished.
- **Casimir** — the whole reason he came to the Amber Temple was to resurrect her, which he had not told anyone. Now visibly corpse-like and apparently unaware of it. Still travelling with the party.
- **Two nothics** *(friendly)* — cursed wizards drawn to concentrated magic. Identified by Dablin on an arcana check. Encouraged the party to take the gifts.

## Fallen
No deaths. The resurrection gift is now in the party's hands for the first time, which may matter later.

## Cliffhanger / where we left off
Standing in front of two more dark gifts — one "of ice and of hate," one of secrets — with two friendly nothics telling them to go ahead. Behind them: an untouched treasure hoard guarded by an amber golem, a second golem someone already destroyed, and Rahadin walking away with a truce that lasts exactly as long as nobody swings. Phoenix Cloak is carrying a one-shot resurrection and a −4 to everything he cannot clear without a long rest he cannot take.
