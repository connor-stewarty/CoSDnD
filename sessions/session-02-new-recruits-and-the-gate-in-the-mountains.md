# Session 02 — New Recruits and the Gate in the Mountains

**Date played:** 2026-08-13
**Real-world session length:** ~172 minutes recorded (~2h52m)
**In-game date:** Picks up back in Barovia village at 2pm; covers that afternoon, a night camped on the road, a full day's travel by carriage (including a second night camped in/on the carriage), and into the following day at a mountain gate.

## TL;DR
Back in Barovia village, the party recruited two more allies for the fight against Strahd — a sold-off strongman and a legendary, grieving vampire hunter — survived an ambush of animated cursed weapons at a bridge, stole the ambushers' carriage, and fought their way through a gate of animated guardian statues on the road toward the Amber Temple.

## What happened
- Session opens back in Barovia village, 2pm, picking up from Session 01's cliffhanger. The DM flags this arc as approaching "borderline a point of no return."
- The party recalls an old prophecy from an earlier reading (predating this log): *"A young man with a kind heart, a mother's boy — strong of body but weak in mind. Seek him in the village of Barovia."*
- Walking toward the tavern, they see a huge, muscular man get thrown out of a shop along with a companion, apparently for having no money — a good early guess for the prophecy's target.
- The tavern itself is desolate and price-gouging: the only things for sale are water, vinegar, and "spicy water" (sand-flavored water), at absurd hyperinflated prices (2 gold for a glass of water — "it's like $200"). Bulbin drinks his own liquor rather than pay. Phoenix Cloak lifts the bartender's last bottle of wine — the only real alcohol left in the village — via sleight of hand while she's distracted haggling. The party asks about the prophecy's "kind but easily bossed-around" young man and gets a name and a shop to check: **Parriwimple**.
- At the shop, everything is marked up roughly 10x (a walking staff: 50gp; an antitoxin: 500gp). Casimir warns the party that the road ahead climbs into extreme cold — he'll be fine (he has a ring that protects against cold), but the rest of the party should gear up. After a gold/silver/copper mix-up joke, the party buys one blanket for 5gp.
- Parriwimple — the huge man from earlier — turns out to be the shopkeeper's own son. She offers to sell his services to the party outright: 75 gold to take him along and let him die gloriously fighting Strahd, since a Strahd-free Barovia means more customers for her shop. A persuasion check (with advantage) lands the deal at 75 gold. Parriwimple turns out not to be the barbarian the party assumed — mechanically, he's an NPC with no class, "just attacks once per turn." He immediately asks the party to pull Strahd's teeth after they kill him, to sell as necklaces.
- On the road out of town (a custom route the DM built instead of random encounters), the party reaches dusk near the windmill and chooses to camp rather than risk arriving there at midnight, too close to Strahd's castle. At camp, Parriwimple bare-hands a tree down for firewood ("this guy is crazy"), then spends a solid ten minutes failing to start the fire with pure muscle before finally managing it — the table's ongoing "birth of the first Super Saiyan" bit. Casimir and Dablin trade spells: Casimir gives up **Suggestion**, Dablin gives up **Lightning Bolt**.
- The next morning, Casimir advises against the direct mountain route to the Amber Temple — too steep, avalanche risk — and the party takes the standard road instead.
- On the road, they spot what looks like an ambushed carriage at a bridge: an injured man on the ground (turns out to be fake blood — a trap), two horses, and goods in the back. Investigating triggers an ambush: animated cursed weapons (blades acting like living things) erupt from the water and the carriage, clearly leftover magic from a cultist/mage who'd died there and dumped his remaining power into the weapons. Casimir gets caught off guard early and takes a hard hit, but the party clears the fight without anyone going down. As the last weapon starts to fade, Irina manages to grab it before it disappears — the party can turn it into a permanent +2 weapon or a summonable spirit ally; they choose the weapon, reforge it down into a **+2 short sword**, and give it to Phoenix Cloak.
- Loot from the bridge fight: the carriage (with two horses, functional after some love), one broom, and two damaged sets of plate armor. Dog and Bulbin carve stakes from the broom, reasoning "this will be the weapon that murders Strahd."
- Recurring bit born here: Parriwimple's self-declared spell, "**Power Word Poo**" — used at least twice this session, including once specifically to lighten the overloaded carriage.
- The party commandeers the carriage — Bulbin drives, on the strength of having proficiency with land vehicles. On the road, a precisely aimed bolt of radiant light disables a wheel. The shooter: **Rudolph van Richten**, an aged but legendary (semi-retired) vampire hunter, furious and grieving — he accuses the party of killing his daughter. An arcana check reveals he's already cast Death Ward on himself, expecting a fight. Dablin talks him down with a strong persuasion roll, and a check on Rudolph van Richten's holy symbol confirms his daughter, **Esmeralda**, made a pact with the party to help kill Strahd before she died — in the same fight (before this log begins) that killed Cassius. Once Rudolph van Richten understands she died an ally rather than a victim, he asks to join for vengeance instead, and the party agrees. He immediately starts casting **Mending** on the badly overloaded carriage wheel, which needs recasting roughly every two miles.
- The party bypasses Vallaki on the scenic route (just past noon) and passes the site where they'd earlier killed one of Rudolph van Richten's tigers — an awkward beat that gets glossed over. Rudolph van Richten gifts them the tiger's pelt anyway; Dog wears it for warmth. A hit-and-run on a bear along the road nets a second pelt. Rudolph van Richten shares some of his history and offers to teach vampire-hunting technique from a hand-written, 500-page book he carries.
- As night and the cold set in again, the party parks the carriage and piles in together for warmth rather than risk driving in the dark — Parriwimple alone takes up the entire floor of the carriage.
- The next day, the party reaches a stone gate on the mountain road toward the Amber Temple: old, snow-capped, ringed with small vulture-like statues mounted on spikes. No traps or hidden mechanisms are found on inspection — it's a simple iron grate gate. Parriwimple and Dog lift it open together.
- The moment the gate opens, the statues animate: two guardian creatures peel off the wall, and two more arrive through screaming portal-tears, apparently summoned by a hidden mage-guardian's magic. A long fight follows. Highlights: Scottishbard's Dissonant Whispers panics one guardian into fleeing outright; a fairy fire mishap blinds half the party's own side along with the enemy; a poison-gas attack from one guardian poisons most of the party at once; Dog lands a natural 20 crit that somehow rolls the worst possible damage (three 1s and a 2 — "the worst crit I've ever done"); Phoenix Cloak discovers, mid-fight, that he has Uncanny Dodge; Scottishbard's Vicious Mockery ("your mom") one-shots a summoned guardian outright, which also yanks its still-linked twin back through its portal; the last guardian standing is finished off by a thrown spear from Parriwimple.
- Right after the fight, Rudolph van Richten heals Scottishbard's stacked poison damage with holy water — moments before Scottishbard's own saving throw would have cleared it anyway. Session ends here, gate held open, the party not yet through it.

## Key decisions
- Paid 75 gold to recruit Parriwimple outright rather than walk away — he matched the prophecy, and extra muscle (even classless muscle) felt worth it heading into harder fights.
- Took the standard road instead of the steep direct mountain route to the Amber Temple, on Casimir's advice, to avoid avalanche risk.
- Chose to hear Rudolph van Richten out and confirm Esmeralda's pact rather than fight a furious, Death-Ward'd, legendary vampire hunter — turned a near-fight into a recruit.
- Reforged the captured cursed weapon into a +2 short sword for Phoenix Cloak rather than keeping it as a longsword or a summonable ally.

## Combat / danger
- **Bridge ambush** — animated cursed weapons (erupting from water and a decoy wrecked carriage) attacked the party and a surprised Casimir. No one went down; the party captured and repurposed the last weapon before it could vanish.
- **The mountain gate** — two animated guardian statues plus two summoned reinforcements. The whole party got poisoned by a gas attack, Dablin took a hard hit early (bloodied), and a huge crit from Dog was wasted on terrible damage dice, but the party cleared the gate without any deaths or drops.

## Loot & discoveries
- **+2 short sword** — reforged from a captured animated cursed weapon at the bridge; given to Phoenix Cloak.
- A carriage with two horses — the party's new transportation.
- One broom and two damaged plate armor sets, also from the bridge.
- Tiger and bear pelts — cold-weather gear for the mountain road.
- Dablin traded away **Suggestion** and picked up **Lightning Bolt** from Casimir.
- Confirmation from Rudolph van Richten's holy symbol that his daughter **Esmeralda** made a pact with the party to help kill Strahd before dying in "the house" — possibly tied to Session 01's still-unresolved mystery of who dealt the radiant damage transcribed as "Gerland." Worth asking the DM directly.

## NPCs encountered
- **Parriwimple** *(new ally, no class — NPC)* — a huge, kind, easily-bossed-around strongman; sold to the party by his own mother in Barovia village for 75 gold, matching an old prophecy. Chops trees bare-handed, wants Strahd's teeth as trophies.
- **Rudolph van Richten** *(new ally)* — an aged, legendary (semi-retired) vampire hunter and priest-type support (Mending, Holy Water); Esmeralda's father. Attacked the party first, believing them responsible for her death; joined once he learned she died an ally.
- **Esmeralda** *(mentioned, not present)* — Rudolph van Richten's late daughter; a vampire hunter who made a pact with the party before dying in "the house," alongside Cassius.
- **Irina** — the Vallaki inn ally from Session 01, now traveling on the road with the party; grabbed the surviving cursed weapon at the bridge before it could disappear.
