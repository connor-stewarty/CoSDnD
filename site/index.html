The Story So Far

About a year into the campaign, fresh out of a house that cost them people, the party regrouped in Vallaki, picked up two new allies, and got their first real lead on Strahd's weakness: the Amber Temple. A dusk elf wizard named Casimir has joined the cause and knows the way. The plan now is Barovia village, more allies, then a one-way trip into old, guarded, unfriendly magic.
