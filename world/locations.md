# Locations Explored

Barovia is a small, closed valley — this is our map of it, built up only from where we've actually been.

| Location | First visited (session) | What we found | Still unexplored / to return to |

| Vallaki (inn) | 01 | Home base for this session; Irina and Sushura are allies here. Town was mid pro-Strahd "celebration" per prep notes. | — |
| The windmill | before 01 (~2 in-game weeks before session 01) | Killed a coven of three hags here. | — |
