# Locations Explored

Barovia is a small, closed valley — this is our map of it, built up only from where we've actually been.

| Location | First visited (session) | What we found | Still unexplored / to return to |
|---|---|---|---|
| "The house" (pre-session 01) | before 01 | Lost party members here to something ghoul-like, not true vampires — extremely tanky (~200 HP), self-healing unless burned or hit with radiant damage. Exact identity/name TBD. | Note the real name once we place it. |
| Vallaki (inn) | 01 | Home base for this session; Irina and Sushura are allies here. Town was mid pro-Strahd "celebration" per prep notes. | — |
| The windmill | before 01 (~2 in-game weeks before session 01) | Killed a coven of three hags here. | — |
| Vistani / Dusk Elf camp (near Vallaki) | 01 | Recruited Casimir; learned the dusk elves' history with Strahd. | Only met the elder guard and Casimir — the Vistani themselves not yet approached directly. |
| Amber Temple | 01 (lead) / 03 (arrived) | Built into the mountainside, ~50 feet high, six enclaves of praying hooded statues along its face. Entrance hall cleared in 03. **Upper floor cleared in 04**: a barracks (berserkers, gladiators, a dire wolf), a storeroom, a potion workshop, a tool workshop holding a replica of Strahd's castle, a dining room that was an illusion over specters, and a skull room with a statue that charms you into starving. It is **−10°C inside** — Constitution saves every 60 minutes of in-fiction time, and every search costs 5 minutes off that clock. Per the DM, this place "loves mages." | The level below, reached by a staircase found with the Wand of Secrets — and the **sarcophagi** the lich says are down there. Several doors on the upper floor, and the lich's library. |
| Barovia (village) | 02 | Recruited Parriwimple here; hyperinflated prices at the tavern and general store (2gp for water, goods marked up ~10x). | — |
| Bridge on the road to the Amber Temple | 02 | Ambushed by animated cursed weapons at a decoy wrecked carriage; captured the carriage (with two horses) and a reforged +2 short sword. Also where Rudolph van Richten first confronted the party. | Exact name TBD. |
| "The Statue Gate" (mountain road to the Amber Temple) | 02 | Old stone gate ringed with vulture-like statues that animate as guardians; fought through it, gate now open. Snow, extreme cold, steep terrain — Casimir warned against the direct route nearby due to avalanche risk. | Gone through in Session 03 — the tower below is on the far side. |
| The tower past the gate | 03 | A stone tower just beyond the statue gate, with a broken bridge outside it. Kicked the door in and long-rested here; the first floor is much warmer than the rest. A frozen trap door leads to a top floor full of stone statues of soldiers in full plate armor — nothing suspicious about them beyond the fact that the last set of statues also looked harmless. | Real name TBD. Irina and Rudolph van Richten are still here, with a fire going — a fallback point on the way back down. |
| Exethanter's room & library (Amber Temple, upper floor) | 04 | Behind a secret door across the pit. Home of the lich, who is friendly and remembers nothing. His spell book (Abyssal, nauseating, jealously guarded) sits in the study. The library's books crumble to dust when touched — except one, which gave Dablin permanent comprehension of all languages. | Nothing obvious left, though the lich says there are more dark gifts all over the temple. |
| The pit below the skull room (Amber Temple) | 04 | Opened when the ceiling chest was forced and the floor disintegrated. Dark, full of broken swords, and stocked with poltergeists. Bulbin fell 30 feet into it and cleared it alone. | A door down there that looked open — never explored. |
| The sarcophagus vault (Amber Temple, lower level) | 05 | Down the stairs from the library. Three **amber sarcophagi** radiating magic, guarded by vampire spawn that climbed out of them. Touching one shows you a vision and a yes/no prompt — you can decline. Seen so far: the **Dark Gift of the Vampire** (blood, a cave of teeth — declined), the **Gift of Tenebrous** (life and death — declined), and a one-time **Resurrection** from "the Corpse Star," taken by Casimir and then by Scottishbard at the cost of −4 to everything until a long rest. | More gifts are scattered throughout the temple, per Exethanter. |
| The amber golem hoard (Amber Temple, lower level) | 05 | Piles of treasure — 17,000 copper, 12,000 silver, electrum, gems, jewels, ingots, silvered weapons, six fine dresses, 30 holy symbols, a gilded skull, an obsidian scepter, a gilded chariot — guarded by a **giant golem made of amber, invisible** until someone cast See Invisibility. Dog found it by walking into it. The party took nothing. A **second amber golem** elsewhere on this level has already been destroyed by persons unknown. | All of it. Nobody has worked out how to take any of it without waking the golem. |
