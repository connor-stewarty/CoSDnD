# Locations Explored

Barovia is a small, closed valley — this is our map of it, built up only from where we've actually been.

| Location | First visited (session) | What we found | Still unexplored / to return to |
|---|---|---|---|
| "The house" (pre-session 01) | before 01 | Lost party members here to something ghoul-like, not true vampires — extremely tanky (~200 HP), self-healing unless burned or hit with radiant damage. Exact identity/name TBD. | Note the real name once we place it. |
| Vallaki (inn) | 01 | Home base for this session; Irina and Sushura are allies here. Town was mid pro-Strahd "celebration" per prep notes. | — |
| The windmill | before 01 (~2 in-game weeks before session 01) | Killed a coven of three hags here. | — |
| Vistani / Dusk Elf camp (near Vallaki) | 01 | Recruited Casimir; learned the dusk elves' history with Strahd. | Only met the elder guard and Casimir — the Vistani themselves not yet approached directly. |
| Amber Temple | 01 (lead) / 03 (arrived) | Built straight into the mountainside, ~50 feet high, with about six enclaves along its face, each holding a small statue of a hooded figure at prayer. Stairways up the middle, rubble everywhere, savage cold, the road buried in snow. The entrance hall has arrow slits on both floors and a 40-foot eyeless statue at the end — which turned out to be a spellcasting guardian. Per the DM, this place "loves mages." | Everything past the entrance hall. A second-floor door with six armed strangers behind it, a hallway with a burnt corpse and another set of doors past that, and a wolf's den reachable through a broken gap in the outer wall. |
| Barovia (village) | 02 | Recruited Parriwimple here; hyperinflated prices at the tavern and general store (2gp for water, goods marked up ~10x). | — |
| Bridge on the road to the Amber Temple | 02 | Ambushed by animated cursed weapons at a decoy wrecked carriage; captured the carriage (with two horses) and a reforged +2 short sword. Also where Rudolph van Richten first confronted the party. | Exact name TBD. |
| "The Statue Gate" (mountain road to the Amber Temple) | 02 | Old stone gate ringed with vulture-like statues that animate as guardians; fought through it, gate now open. Snow, extreme cold, steep terrain — Casimir warned against the direct route nearby due to avalanche risk. | Gone through in Session 03 — the tower below is on the far side. |
| The tower past the gate | 03 | A stone tower just beyond the statue gate, with a broken bridge outside it. Kicked the door in and long-rested here; the first floor is much warmer than the rest. A frozen trap door leads to a top floor full of stone statues of soldiers in full plate armor — nothing suspicious about them beyond the fact that the last set of statues also looked harmless. | Real name TBD. Irina and Rudolph van Richten are still here, with a fire going — a fallback point on the way back down. |
