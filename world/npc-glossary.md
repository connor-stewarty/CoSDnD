| Exethanter *(the lich)* | 04 | Amber Temple, upper floor, behind a secret door | Calls himself the front desk librarian — answers questions, doesn't guide | Not hostile | Rotting face, flaming eyes, small, entirely friendly. **Name learned in 05**, read off his own spell book once Dablin could comprehend all languages — it also opens at least one arcane-locked door in the temple. Remembers almost nothing: "I have Alzheimer's." **Took one of the temple's dark gifts himself — "and look how I turned out."** Gave the party permission to go below and made Bulbin's great axe +1 for a good persuasion roll. His spell book is in Abyssal, makes people vomit on sight, and he would "probably try to murder" anyone who grabbed it. Plays bingo alone; the voices call the numbers. |
| Barracks berserkers & gladiators | 03 (seen) / 04 (fought) | Amber Temple, second floor off the entrance hall | Garrison of some kind — were sharpening weapons | Hostile (killed) | The six-plus armed strangers Bulbin shut the door on in Session 03. Fought alongside a dire wolf. Per the DM, they *should* have joined the guardian fight in 03 and he held them back because the party would have died. |
| Rahadin | 05 | Amber Temple, lower hall, praying to a statue | Strahd's first servant; "biggest fan of Strahd" | Hostile — truce only | Part dusk elf, something vampiric. Eats live rats. **Personally ratted out the dusk elves and had them massacred, and was promoted for it** — Casimir called him "betrayer" to his face. Already knew the party's plan ("well known inside our networks"); bats carry word. Offered a mutual truce: he won't start a fight if they don't. Insight (22) read him as beatable at full strength but likely to cost a life right now. The party let him walk. |
| Casimir's sister | 05 | Amber Temple, sarcophagus vault | Unknown — conscious for about thirty seconds | Unknown | A **female dusk elf**, which the party was told shouldn't exist. | The nothics | 05 | Amber Temple, lower level | Drawn to concentrated magic; told the party to go ahead and take the gifts | Friendly | Identified by Dablin on an arcana check (26) as **cursed wizards** reduced to this state by something they did.
